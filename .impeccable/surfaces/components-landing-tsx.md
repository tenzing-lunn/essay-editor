# Landing + sample (front door)

Scope: `/` signed out (components/Landing.tsx) and `/sample` (components/SampleDemo.tsx). Mode: Persuade (landing), Operate-lite (sample demo). Audience: a student with a draft due; job: see that Scholia marks and never rewrites, then try it. Action: Try it on a sample / Check my own essay. Proof: the real sample essay and its precomputed marks. No invented users, logos, or numbers.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The essay sits on a ruled notebook page; Scholia's notes live in the wide margin, each tied to its words by a pen line. Refuses the SaaS hero-plus-screenshot.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white ruled paper, blue rules, pink margin rule, blue-black ballpoint ink, soft layered highlighters (multiply), small pen sigla linking mark to note, graphite secondary text. Literata typed, Figtree UI, Kalam pen.
STORY: The visitor sees a real paragraph marked up, reads one note, understands "notes, not rewrites", clicks Try it on a sample.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Page heading (promise) top left on the ruled grid with a highlighter swipe; buttons under it; sample paragraph below on the rules; three margin notes right of the pink rule, pen lines to their phrases; a pen gloss on the name beside the wordmark.
FORM: The Scholia Page, list position 3, seed 4fa980b4. Signature: opening a note dims the rest and redraws its pen line; severity as pen weight.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
