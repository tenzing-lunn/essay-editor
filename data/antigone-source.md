# Antigone source

`antigone.xml` is an unmodified copy of the Perseus Digital Library's modernized
Richard Jebb translation of Sophocles' *Antigone* (Cambridge University Press, 1891).
Downloaded October 8, 2026. The original TEI header and attribution are preserved.

Source: https://github.com/PerseusDL/canonical-greekLit/blob/master/data/tlg0011/tlg002/tlg0011.tlg002.perseus-eng2.xml

Viewer: https://scaife.perseus.org/reader/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0011.tlg002.perseus-eng2/

The bundled XML has not been modified. Lookup results normalize whitespace and
omit stage directions; displayed citations cover the complete translated blocks.
